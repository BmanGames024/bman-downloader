Welcome to BmanDownloader, a small project dedicated for Xbox 360 games conversion and acquirement

Current Features include
Downloading and auto converting Xbox 360 games into God format or XeX format,
Auto upload the content to your console via FTP
Bulk convert isos to God format or XeX format
Install Xefu files correctly





Download the exe and open it (may take up to 1min to load as its caching Archive.org to load easier on next load)


You are going to need to get your account info from Archive.org, using inspect element and navigating to the cookies (different every browser) you need your login user and sig, to connect your account

If you plan on uploading via FTP make sure you put your console info in the FTP section of settings (FTP features do not show up unless the app has made a connection to the console for FTP)

Other then that, the app is ready to go
